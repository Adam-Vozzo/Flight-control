    How to play

    1. Draw a flight path. Touch an aircraft and drag. It follows your line.
    2. Match the colours. Planes land on their runway, from the arrow end. Helicopters land on the pad, from any side.
    3. Keep them apart. A red ring means two aircraft are too close. One collision ends the game.
